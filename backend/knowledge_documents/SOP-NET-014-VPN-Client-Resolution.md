# VPN Client Connection Troubleshooting
Category: Network & VPN
Approval: Assessment sample procedure; an organization should replace this with its approved internal SOP before production use.

## Scope
Use this checklist when an employee reports that the corporate VPN will not connect or disconnects unexpectedly. Do not disable endpoint security, bypass multi-factor authentication, share credentials, or change production network settings.

## Steps
1. Confirm the employee has a working internet connection by opening an approved public company page.
2. Ask the employee to record the exact VPN error text and the time it occurred. Never ask for a password, MFA code, private key, or session token.
3. Confirm the device date and time are set automatically and that the approved VPN client is running.
4. Ask the employee to disconnect and reconnect once using the organization's normal sign-in flow.
5. If the problem persists, collect the VPN client version, operating system, connection type and error message for the network support team.
6. Escalate repeated authentication failures, certificate warnings, suspected compromise or multi-user outages to the network/security team. Do not change tunnel MTU or security policy without an authorized runbook and approval.

## Escalation
Escalate if connectivity remains unavailable after the checks above, if the VPN reports certificate/security errors, or if multiple employees are affected.
