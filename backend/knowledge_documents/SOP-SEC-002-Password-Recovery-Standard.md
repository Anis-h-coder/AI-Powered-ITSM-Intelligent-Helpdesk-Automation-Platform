# Password Expiration and Account Recovery
Category: Identity & Access
Approval: Assessment sample procedure; replace with the organization's approved identity policy before production use.

## Steps
1. Use only the company's official self-service password reset or identity portal reached from a known company bookmark.
2. Complete the configured identity verification steps. IT support must not request or record the employee's current password, new password, MFA code, recovery code or session token.
3. If the identity portal reports that the account is locked, use the approved account recovery process; do not bypass MFA or issue a temporary credential without verified authorization.
4. After a successful reset, sign in through the normal company sign-in page and update credentials only in approved applications.
5. If identity verification fails, the account is privileged, or repeated MFA prompts are unexpected, stop and escalate to Identity & Access Management.

## Safety
This guide does not authorize an automated password reset or account unlock. The assessment application may simulate a proposed action but must not change real credentials.
